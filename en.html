Privacy Policy

App: Imperio de Barrio: Idle
Data controller: Nejosudo
Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how Imperio de Barrio handles data. The game can be played offline and without creating a password account. If you do not use Play Games, your progress stays on your device.

1. Data we process

  • Game progress and settings. Money, buildings, upgrades, brand, achievements, skins, language, volumes, and preferences. They are stored locally on your device. If you enable Play Games backup, a copy is also stored in Google's cloud.
  • Google Play Games Services. If your Google account is available, the game may access your player ID, public display name, and avatar, and use Play Games saved games to back up and restore your progress. This connection is optional: the game works locally without it.
  • In-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your card details. We may receive the product ID, purchase state, and order ID to deliver and restore your purchases.
  • Advertising. We use Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial ads. AdMob may process your Android advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information, language, and ad interaction data. Personalized ads are shown only if you consent through the consent form (UMP). You can change your ad preferences in Android settings.
  • Analytics and crashes. We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand which features are used and fix bugs. These services may process installation identifiers, usage events, device model, Android version, app version, and crash logs. We do not use this data to identify you personally.
  • Notifications. Alerts (offline earnings cap and Rush Hour) are local device notifications. They are not sent from a server we operate.
  • Support. If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in the message.

2. How we use data

  • Save and restore your game, including cloud backup.
  • Deliver and restore purchases and prevent fraud.
  • Show ads, measure their performance, and respect your consent choices.
  • Understand game usage, improve balance, and fix errors.
  • Respond to your support requests.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be processed by the Google services required for the game to work: Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics. You can read Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We may also share information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the game.

4. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Play Games backup remains in your Google account until you delete it from Play Games settings or ask us for help. Analytics and crash data are retained according to the periods configured in Firebase. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the request.

5. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect information. Local saves include a backup copy and integrity checks. No system is 100% infallible, but we do not store passwords or payment card data.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, or portability of your data, and withdraw your consent for personalized ads, by emailing nejosudo2024@gmail.com. You can also clear local data in Android settings and manage your Play Games account through Google. If you live in the European Economic Area, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

8. International transfers

Google services may process data outside your country, using the safeguards Google applies to international transfers. See Google's policy for details.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when game features or the law change. We will publish the current version on this page and show the update date. If the change is important, we will notify you in the game.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights: nejosudo2024@gmail.com.
